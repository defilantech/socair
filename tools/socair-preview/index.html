Socair preview

Put socair.ai/ in front of a Hugging Face model URL. Your browser runs Socair's open engine on the model's headers and small files, fetched straight from Hugging Face. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and no account is needed.

What this preview is, and is not
  • Unsigned. It is your browser's run of the engine, not an attestation, and Socair vouches for nothing here.
  • Headers only. It reads each weight file's header, not its weights. Checks that need the weights are left for a full scan.
  • Hashes as Hugging Face states them for the large files; the small files' hashes come from their bytes.
  • No decision. Whether this model is approved is your organization's call, under its own policy and its own key.
