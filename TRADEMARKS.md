## Without asking

- Use, change, and redistribute the code under Apache-2.0.
- Refer to the project by name in articles, talks, tutorials, and
  comparisons.
- Say truthfully that your product or service uses, works with, or is
  compatible with Socair, for example "verifies Socair attestations" or
  "works with Socair".
- Run Socair releases in your organization and say so.

## Use a different name

- A fork, or a modified build you distribute, must not be called Socair or
  use the logo. Give it its own name; you may say it is "based on Socair".
- Do not use the name or the logo in a way that suggests Defilan Technologies
  makes, endorses, or issues something it does not. In particular, an
  attestation is issued by whoever signs it, not by Defilan
  ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).
- Do not use "Socair" as part of the name of your own product, company, or
  domain.
