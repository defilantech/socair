# Socair: brand note

## The name

- Word: Socair
- Language: Irish
- Meaning: at ease, settled, secure
- Approximate pronunciation: SUH-ker

Socair is the Irish word for being at ease and settled. The name describes the outcome the product works toward rather than the mechanism, and carries a deliberate tension: serene surface over serious assurance.

It needs no diacritics (unlike Irish words such as seala or Slovak words such as zaruka that require a fada or an accent), so it is the same word in a domain, a logo, and a terminal.

## Positioning

Socair is an open-source project from Defilan Technologies, under
Apache-2.0, with no paid edition. The trademark keeps the name: anyone may use,
change, and ship the code, but only the project ships something called Socair.

LLMKube remains the open-source serving platform. Socair carries its own name
under Defilan, not an LLMKube prefix, which leaves room for a product family
(for example an artifact registry or an airlock appliance under the same name).

## Logo

The mark is a **cairn**: three stones, balanced, the top one just settled.
*Cairn* comes from the Gaelic *carn*. A cairn marks a safe path and is literally
settled stone, which is the name's meaning: at ease, settled, secure. The
wordmark is a monoline, lowercase `socair` drawn from geometric strokes, with
no font dependency.

Use:
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the top stone's height.
- The accent belongs to the top stone only.
- Do not recolor the stones individually, rotate the mark, add effects, or set
  the wordmark in a typeface.
- At 32 px and below, use the tile or the mark without the wordmark.
