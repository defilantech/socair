# Socair: brand note

## The name

- Word: Socair
- Language: Irish
- Meaning: at ease, settled, secure
- Approximate pronunciation: SUH-ker

Socair is the Irish word for being at ease and settled. It names the outcome a security buyer is paying for: the ability to stop worrying about a model that is about to land on the corporate network. The name carries a deliberate tension, serene surface over serious assurance, rather than describing the mechanism.

The word sits naturally for a company started by a Maher, and the story is told in one sentence: Socair is the Irish word for secure and at ease, and it is what we give a CISO before a model is served.

It needs no diacritics (unlike Irish words such as seala or Slovak words such as zaruka that require a fada or an accent), so it is the same word in a domain, a logo, and a terminal.

## Trademark status

A search of the trademark system returned no results for Socair. Scope and caveats, stated so this note is not read as clearance:

- The result covers registered and pending marks in the system searched. It does not cover common-law or unregistered use, state registrations, or foreign marks.
- The search was for the exact string. Near marks and phonetic variants were not verified.
- The goods and services classes that matter for this product are US class 9 (software) and class 42 (software services, security). Confirm these were in scope.
- If the product is sold in the EU, UK, or Ireland (relevant given the Irish name), run EUIPO, UKIPO, and the Irish register as well.

Status: clear enough to build a private brand and a private repo on, not bulletproof.

## Positioning

Socair is a Defilan Technologies product. LLMKube remains the open-source platform and stays open forever; Socair is the paid enterprise assurance product and carries its own name under Defilan, not an LLMKube prefix. This leaves room for a product family later (for example an artifact registry or an airlock appliance under the same name).
